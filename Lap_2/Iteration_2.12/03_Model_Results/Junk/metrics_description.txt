Hydrology Metrics Description
==============================

Kling-Gupta Efficiency (KGE)
Description: Combines correlation, bias, variability.
Range: (-∞,1]
Units: Dimensionless
Interpretation: Higher better
------------------------------

Normalized KGE (NKGE)
Description: Normalized form of KGE
Range: (0,1]
Units: Dimensionless
Interpretation: Higher better
------------------------------

Nash-Sutcliffe Efficiency (NSE)
Description: Variance explained
Range: (-∞,1]
Units: Dimensionless
Interpretation: Higher better
------------------------------

Percent Bias (PBIAS)
Description: Mean bias
Range: (-∞,∞)
Units: %
Interpretation: Closer to 0 better
------------------------------

Root Mean Square Error (RMSE)
Description: Error magnitude
Range: [0,∞)
Units: units
Interpretation: Lower better
------------------------------

Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
Description: Absolute error
Range: [0,∞)
Units: units
Interpretation: Lower better
------------------------------

Coefficient of Determination (R2)
Description: Variance explained
Range: [0,1]
Units: Dimensionless
Interpretation: Higher better
------------------------------

Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE)
Description: Relative error
Range: [0,∞)
Units: %
Interpretation: Lower better
------------------------------

Volume Error (VE)
Description: Volumetric bias
Range: (-∞,∞)
Units: %
Interpretation: Closer to 0 better
------------------------------
